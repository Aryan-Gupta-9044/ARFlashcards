# AR Flashcards (Unity 6 + Vuforia, Android)

Point your phone at a printed animal card: a 3D animal appears on the card, with its name, a fact, sound and voice
(English / Hindi / Tamil), plus a quiz. If AR cannot start, the app falls back to a 3D viewer, so the screen is never empty.

**Start here: `docs/START_HERE.md`** (10 steps, from unzip to the app on your phone).

* Open this folder in Unity Hub (Add > Add project from disk). Unity 6 (tested design target 6000.x).
* No packages are needed to compile. Vuforia is installed by the menu `AR Flashcards > Setup Wizard`.
* Print `Cards/PRINT_ME_A4.pdf` (5 cards, 90 mm wide).
